Why we exist

Committees change. We don't.

Every society elects a new committee each year. The contacts, the half-planned events and the goodwill with sponsors often leave with the old one, so partners start from scratch.

Open Build holds the relationship in the middle. New committees inherit a partner who already knows them, and partners keep one contact across years.

How a collab works

Three steps, then it repeats.

1

Tell us what you want

An audience, a tool to teach, a problem to hack on, or people to hire. Two lines is enough to start.

2

We bring the right societies

We match you with societies whose members fit, line up rooms and dates, and share the plan with every society in the network.

3

We keep it going

After the event we hand the next committee the contacts and notes, so the collab runs again next year without starting over.
