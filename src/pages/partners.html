How it works.

  1. Tell us who you want to reach.

  2. We find the societies.

  3. We run it with you.

    We help plan, promote and run the day, and we're still your contact next year.

Committees change. We don't.

Every society elects a new committee each year, and most partnerships leave with the old one. We keep the contacts and the history, so next year's committee starts with partners, not an empty inbox.
